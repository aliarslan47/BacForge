BacForge Pipeline DAG

Bacterial WGS analysis pipeline, M00–M18. Shape: branch → merge → fan → merge → report. The platform decision (short·long·hybrid) picks tools at M01/M03 and merges at the M04 genome.fasta hub; from there independent enrichment modules fan out, are gathered at M17 and rejoin in the M18 report. Edges are extracted from the real run() access sites.
