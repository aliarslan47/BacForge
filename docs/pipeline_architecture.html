BacForge Pipeline DAG

The real run() dependency graph. The platform decision is made in M00 (short / long / hybrid / assembly); M01 and M03 branch internally by platform and rejoin at M04. M04 (the polished genome) is the central hub — M05–M16 fan out from it. M17 aggregates all analysis outputs and M18 is the report capstone. Conditional modules (M13–M16) are stamped N/A when inputs are absent.
